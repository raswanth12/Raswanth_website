01 About

Where capability meets the business case.

Most AI conversations stall in the gap between what a platform can do and what a business will pay for. That gap is where I work.

At MiPhi Semiconductors — a Phison joint venture — I translate enterprise requirements into AI infrastructure and system architectures across automotive, government and enterprise accounts. A single day can move from an engineering deep-dive, to an RFP evaluation board, to a CXO pipeline review — and my job is to make all three conversations point the same direction.

The technical foundation is real: deep learning and computer vision research at Tata Institute of Social Sciences, CNN and reinforcement learning perception models, geospatial pipelines built in PyTorch. I use it every day. It's the difference between demonstrating a product and defending an architecture.
